/about.md

about.md

I'm a software engineer based in Toronto, currently building design infrastructure for teams that ship fast without breaking things. I got into this field by breaking my school's website for fun — I've been making up for it ever since.

My work sits at the seam between engineering and design: I care about type scales and load times in equal measure. I've led front-end architecture at two Series B startups, mentored junior engineers, and shipped products used by millions of people who will never know my name — which is exactly how it should be.

Off-screen: analog film photography, long-distance running, and an unreasonable number of mechanical keyboards.
